Society — Territory ?
Human.s — Biosphere...
Merizotopies

The subject matter — at once historical, economic, social, political, biotic, technical, spatial, architectural… — is an inquiry into the exit from a multi-millennial "Civilising" system. A model that has long undertaken the marginalisation of the Species on the planetary bedrock through the seizure of planetary soil, the eradication or domestication of "the other Living", the increasing degradation of the Biosphere, the deterioration or depletion of abiotic and physical resources, the disruption of the Earth's climate, and more recently the drive to reformat the "inventive" human cognitive faculty into Artificial-Intelligence…

This Essay recounts the concrete ecological and social initiatives undertaken in recent decades across Europe. Initiatives most often developed at the local scale by groups committed to defining and experimenting with a field of life concepts based on the inter-compatibilities of our Species with Terrestrial Life. These life practices, with a low or even "erasable ecological footprint", combined with a "Frugal" conception of existence, within the framework of implementing an Eco-Etho-politics of living environments in the biosphere, lead step by step to revising the uses and functions of built settlements, and to developing spatial concepts of habitation: in mutuality, geo-biocompatibility, itinerancy, and "Merizotopy".

The approach pursued here consists of questioning the historical "Long Time" in the manner of the Amerindians of Chiapas — "Looking backward to move forward" — in order to address the human societal system across a space-time beginning with the formation of Tribute, then Profit, and continuing its work over the last two centuries by devoting itself simultaneously to: the accelerated degradation of ambient biodiversity and the alteration of the planet's climatic parameters, up to creating a "Digital Era" to develop a process of capturing human knowledge aimed at making way for an algorithmic "Artificial-Intelligence"…

The present accident-prone context provides the occasion to question alternatives to the suicidal systemic model now being revealed. Alternatives concerned with finding a compatibility of existence and evolution of the Species with "Mother Earth", in order to shed the neo-civilising models bent on fabricating an avatarised "cosmic Being".

In the present context, at once ecocidal and genocidal, certain human groups across the Continents are working to elaborate concrete forms of "Alternatives" bearing on the modes of dwelling of a multicultural "Human Common" in eco-compatibility, and are even constituting co-evolutionary forms of existence with the whole of the Living Biospheric world… This is what we have chosen to report on in Europe, presenting: Achievements (buildings and associated developments); the supporting framework of scientific research and architectural techniques that enables their development; and the social and cultural dynamics carried by the neighbourhoods — authors, makers, actors determined to forge a "Co-evolutionist" societal paradigm.

To circumvent the ambient state of disinformation destroying mass reflection, it was chosen to distribute this "Essay" via the Net, and under the "Creative Commons" format, which allows for the updating and evolution of data and reflections through exchange between authors, readers, and new contributors. A formula adopted from the outset of the project that allows each Internet user to: Freely copy and distribute the published works (non-proprietary system); participate in correcting any errors; continue contributing data and experiences that help popularise the forms of disengagement from the prevailing politico-financial model; and thus participate in the development and dissemination of pathways for implementing an eco-systemic alternative.

The themes of change explored in these pages are grounded in the forms of reinvention of living space created within neighbourhood dynamics (Blocks and Quarters). Most of the cited experiences have been visited in Northern Europe; many others exist across Europe and on other Continents, yet to be made known and reported, to show that there is no Utopia in this matter but already a change of course.

The Essay is a call for contributions and action. It states facts and formulates hypotheses on the evolution of the life forms of Human Commons co-evolving with the Living as a whole. It is designed to be approached by Internet users navigating a theme linked to one of the four subjects: SOCIETY – TERRITORY – HUMAN.S – BIOSPHERE. The mode of exposition, chosen to develop the viewpoint of an alternative path to that of the Capitalocene, draws on elements of inquiry and observations examined over the Long Historical Time.

Abstract

The text unfolds in two parts. The first part, entitled "In the Thickness of Time" (settlement time – recent times): Sets out the periods during which the societal model of the "Anthropocene" is constituted through trial and error, before addressing the phase of the establishment of "Capitalism". The second part recounts the emergence, from the second half of the twentieth century onward, of a societal dead end marked by the growing impact of the human footprint on the biosphere, and the observation of an advanced degradation of living and geophysical environments. Under the denomination: "Merizotopies", alternatives for exiting the dead end towards a civil society engaged in collegiality are sketched, and eco-compatible forms of evolution between the Human Species and the Living Planet are evoked — a planet that is neither to be bought, nor sold, nor destroyed, but understood.

The "Long Time"… After community gatherings at resource sites, then organisation into denser systems in the form of agglomerated villages, and larger groupings (Market Towns), there emerges — as a result of the social fractures generated by the evolution of forms of production and distribution — the City of "Classes".
It is born in Mesopotamia, around the 5th millennium before our era, at a time characterised by the splitting of human communities along two orientations. The first, driven by a society of "accumulation" characterised by increasing productivity and the accumulation of exchangeable surpluses, and the second, itinerant, seeking compatibility with the climate and equilibrium with the surrounding natural context, but whose development is quickly hindered by the predatory expansion of the first.

Societies developed over millennia will increasingly sophisticate the Model of "Appropriation-Domestication-Extermination", until engaging at the end of the 18th century in a new transformation sustained by access to fossil fuels: That of a development, invasive and destructive of physical and Living environments across the entire planet, sustained by an exponential growth in demography. The more the density of sites of constrained gatherings and exchanges increases, the more the dynamics of communal life disappear in favour of the isolation of the Individual. This phenomenon first appears in modes of production based on Surplus-Tribute, then becomes more sophisticated in "societies of classes and specialisations", and today in systems of digital governance. This last phase seems to accentuate the marginalisation of the Species in relation to bio-diverse and ecosystemic Life.

The "Alternative Path" must instruct humanity on the forms of extrication, deconditioning, and de-addiction from urban "Container" systems, to allow it to reinvent itself into "Generative Commons" conceiving modes and forms of settlements in eco-compatibility with the Living-Planet. Death or Co-living? The urgency demands convincing the Human-Common, by means of the public digital interconnections still in place, to turn towards the biophysical world in order to co-evolve towards forms of mutualisation, weaving of Living Worlds, inter-language, and to position itself at once in reliance with the Living as a whole, and in compatibility with the chemo-bio-geophysical system of the Globe.

The times must give rise to the rapid emergence of a co-evolutionary Living whole, constituted of multiple ecosystemic communities, and of human-commons in discovery, and also "responsible for the upkeep" of a Living Planet within the framework of the astrophysical laws in force. The recent turn towards renewable energy sources and new modes of human access to public intercommunication on a world scale offers the opportunity to connect across long distances, exchange, rethink new fabrics of life in "open collaboration", while striving to recover the biocompatibility of the Species within a planet that is the matrix of Life…

We collectively know how to create these conditions of life with almost no footprint on ecosystemic environments and to coexist with them, we know how to live in sobriety and with low impact on the planet, we know how to reconcile the intimate and the collective within a single architectural unit, we know how to use the renewable resources necessary for our lives without depleting them, we know how to protect the other species of biospheric life… But our productivist anthropo-systemic philosophy has not led us to find a language, an inter-species code…

A wrong turn?
